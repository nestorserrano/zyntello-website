1. What this document is for

No business management platform is written entirely from scratch. Zyntello's rests on free and open source components developed by third parties —the framework the application runs on, the libraries that generate PDFs and Excel files, those that read barcodes, those that draw the charts, and the fonts and icons of the interface—.

Each of those components is distributed under its own licence, and nearly all of those licences require the same thing: that the author's copyright notice be kept and that whoever receives the software knows where it comes from. This document is how Zyntello meets that obligation, and at the same time the answer to a legitimate question from any subscriber: what is the system I trust my accounts to actually built on?

What this document is not. The fact that the Platform incorporates open source components does not make it free software nor grant any right over Zyntello's own code. Each component's licence covers that component and nothing else. The regime of the Platform as a whole is that of section 10 of the Terms of Service: a limited licence of use while the subscription is in force.

Conversely, Zyntello's Terms cannot cut back what a third party's licence grants either. Where the two say different things about a component, the component's licence prevails.

2. How to read the licence table

The licences in this document fall into three families, and the difference between them matters:

  • Permissive (MIT, BSD, ISC, Apache 2.0). They allow the component to be used, modified and redistributed, even inside a commercial product, with one real condition: keeping the copyright notice and the licence text. Apache 2.0 adds an express patent grant and the duty to flag modified files.
  • Weak copyleft (LGPL). They allow the library to be used inside a proprietary product without that product having to be published. What must remain free are the modifications to the library itself. Zyntello uses these libraries without modifying them.
  • Work attribution (CC BY 4.0, SIL OFL). They apply to icons and fonts, not to code. They require crediting the author wherever the work is used. That is why this document exists in the form it does.

No component of the Platform is under strong copyleft (GPL or AGPL) on terms that would require Zyntello's code to be published. Two components are distributed under a dual licence that offers the GPL as an alternative (the nette/* packages, BSD-3-Clause or GPL at the user's choice): Zyntello uses them under the BSD-3-Clause.

6. Components under a commercial licence

Two components of the Platform are not free software. They are declared here for transparency, even though their licence does not require it:

  • Flux, Livewire's interface component library, is distributed under a paid commercial licence. Zyntello uses it under the licence it has purchased. Its code is neither redistributed nor published.
  • QZ Tray, the bridge that allows direct printing to point-of-sale printers and opening the cash drawer, is offered under a dual licence (LGPL-2.1 or a commercial licence). It is a program installed on the Subscriber's device: obtaining it, installing it and, where applicable, licensing it commercially are the Subscriber's own responsibility, who installs it by their own decision and under the provider's terms.

Neither of the two receives or transmits account data to Zyntello by any means other than those already described in the Privacy Policy.

8. How to request a licence text

The full text of the licence of any of the components listed is available on request, without any condition and at no cost. Just write to info@zyntello.com naming the component.

This document is reviewed when the Platform's dependencies change, and in any case at least once a year. A new component, a version that changes licence or the removal of an existing one gives rise to a new version of this document, with its entry in the change log.

If you are the author of any of the components listed and consider the attribution incorrect or insufficient, write to info@zyntello.com and it will be corrected.
