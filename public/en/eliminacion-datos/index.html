User data deletion

This page explains how to request the deletion of your personal data from the Zyntello platform: what you must send, where to, how long it takes to process, what is deleted and what information we must keep by legal obligation.

1. How to request the deletion of your data

Send your request by email

info@zyntello.com

With the subject Data deletion request

Include in the message:

  • your full name;
  • the email address or the phone number you registered with or were contacted on;
  • where applicable, the name of the company that contacted you or recorded your data on the Platform;
  • if you wish, whether you want everything deleted or just one specific item.

You can also send us the request by WhatsApp on +1 829 639 9877. In that case we will ask you to confirm it by email so there is a written record of your identity and your request.

You do not need to have an account. You can request deletion even if you never registered with Zyntello: it is enough that some company using our platform uploaded your data or sent you a communication from it.

To protect your information, we may ask you for reasonable identity verification —for example, that you write from the registered email address or confirm a detail held in the file—. We will not ask you for your password, card details, or documents that are not needed to identify you.

2. Before you ask: identify your situation

Zyntello is a platform other companies use to run their operations. Whose the data is determines who the right is exercised with, so there are two distinct situations:

Your situationWho decides about your dataWhat we do
You are a Zyntello user
You have or had an account, or you are a member of an account's team.		 Zyntello is the controller. We handle and carry out your request directly.
Your data was uploaded by a company
You are a customer, employee, supplier, resident, debtor or participant of a company that uses Zyntello.		 That company is the controller. Zyntello only hosts and processes the data on their behalf. We route your request to that company, tell you so in writing and help them handle it. The final decision is theirs.

If you do not know which situation you are in, write to us anyway. With the email address or phone number you give us we can determine who is responsible for your data and tell you. No request is discarded for being addressed to the wrong party.

3. What happens after your request

  1. Acknowledgement of receipt. We reply confirming that we received the request, normally within 2 working days.
  2. Verification. We check your identity and locate your data. If we need any additional detail to identify you, we ask for it then.
  3. Identifying the controller. If your data was uploaded by a Subscriber company, we contact them and tell you who the request was routed to.
  4. Execution. We delete or block the data as appropriate.
  5. Final written reply. We tell you what was deleted, what was kept and why. Maximum period: 30 days from receipt of the verified request.

The service is free. We do not charge for handling a deletion request, nor do we require any reason for making one.

Deleted data also disappears from our backups once the rotation cycle completes, which is 30 days. During that time it is blocked and is not used for any purpose.

Copies downloaded by the Subscriber company. The Platform lets each Subscriber company download a backup file with their own data. Once downloaded, that file is in the company's hands and beyond Zyntello's reach: we cannot locate or delete it. If your data was uploaded by a Subscriber company, deleting those copies is up to that company, and we will pass your request on to them as described above.

4. What is deleted

Depending on your situation and the scope you request, deletion may cover:

  • your account and sign-in data: name, email, phone, password, roles and permissions;
  • your contact details in the customer, supplier or business contact records;
  • photographs of you or of your property that had been uploaded;
  • geographic location records associated with your activity;
  • notes, comments and management annotations that mention you;
  • your phone number from the messaging lists, together with the record of your opt-out request;
  • your communication preferences and marketing consents.

5. What is kept and why

There is information we cannot delete because the law requires it to be kept or because it is needed to evidence facts. In those cases the data is blocked: its processing is restricted to the legal purpose that justifies keeping it and it is used for nothing else.

About the opt-out record. It may seem contradictory to keep your phone number or email after you ask for deletion, but it is the only way to guarantee you do not receive messages again: if we erased the item entirely, a later upload could include it once more. It is kept solely for the purpose of excluding it.

6. I only want to stop receiving messages

If your aim is not to delete your data but to stop receiving communications, the process is simpler and faster:

  • WhatsApp: reply to the message asking to opt out, or block the number. If the messages come from a company that uses Zyntello, that company must stop sending them immediately. If they do not, write to us and we will take it up with them.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link at the foot of every commercial email.
  • In any case: write to us at info@zyntello.com with the subject Communications opt-out. We process it within a maximum of 72 hours.

Messages sent by Subscriber companies do not originate from Zyntello. Each company is responsible for having your consent and for acting on your opt-out. We provide the tool and the mechanisms to record that preference, and we help when a request is not acted on.

Opting out of commercial communications does not stop strictly transactional messages while a relationship with that company is in force (for example, the invoice for a service you contracted).

7. Other rights and complaints

Besides deletion, you may request access to your data, its rectification, to object to processing, and —where the law provides for it— portability and restriction of processing, as well as withdrawing a consent you gave. The procedure and the deadlines are the same as described here.

The detail of your rights, the rules applicable in each country and the categories of data we process are in the Privacy Policy (sections 9 and 10).

If you believe your request was not properly handled, you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority or the competent court in your country.
