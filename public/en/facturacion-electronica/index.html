What it solves

Invoicing in one system and issuing the e-CF in another

Many companies met the obligation with a separate portal or provider: they invoice in the usual system and then re-enter each invoice to issue the electronic receipt. Every extra step is a chance for the amount, the ITBIS or the customer’s RNC not to match, and for the DGII to reject the document after the goods have already been delivered.

  • Every invoice is typed twice: in the system and in the e-CF portal
  • A DGII rejection is discovered days later
  • Nobody knows which receipts were never transmitted
  • Credit notes are not linked to their invoice
  • The 606 and 607 reports are put together by hand at month-end
  • Suppliers’ e-CFs arrive by email and get lost

What you actually get

It comes from the invoice itself

There is no second system. When the invoice is issued, the e-CF is generated from its data: same amount, same ITBIS, same customer.

Signed and transmitted

The XML is signed with your company’s digital certificate and sent to the DGII, which returns its tracking number.

You know what the DGII answered

Each receipt shows whether it was accepted, conditionally accepted or rejected, and the log keeps every submission and every response.

Printed version and email

The invoice as a PDF with its security code, ready to hand over or email to the customer from the same screen.

Batch retransmission

If the connection dropped or the DGII did not respond, the pending ones are sent again together, without hunting for them one by one.

The e-CFs you receive, too

You import the supplier’s XML, it is validated against the official format, you record its commercial approval or rejection and pre-load it as a purchase.

How it works

In three steps

  1. You upload your digital certificate

    The one the DGII links to your RNC, with your authorized e-CF sequences.

  2. You certify your company

    The DGII tests are passed in its certification environment, on the same system.

  3. You invoice as usual

    Every invoice goes out as an e-CF, signed and transmitted, with its response in view.

In detail

What you notice using it every day

Eight types of receipt

Tax credit (E31), consumer (E32), debit and credit notes (E33 and E34), minor expenses (E43), special regimes (E44), government (E45) and export (E46).

Exports and special regimes, taxed correctly

E44 and E46 are not issued as exempt: they go at the 0% ITBIS rate, which is how the DGII requires them.

Testing, certification and production

The platform works with the DGII’s three environments, so your company’s certification is done on the same system you will invoice with.

Credit notes with their reference

Each note carries the receipt it modifies, as the format requires, and stays linked to the original invoice.

606 and 607 reports

The period’s purchases and sales come from the same receipt register, without copying them by hand into a spreadsheet.

Several companies, separate numbering

Each company in the group uses its own RNC, certificate and sequences, and its receipts never mix with the others’.

Common questions

Questions

Do I need a digital certificate?

Yes. The DGII requires every e-CF to be signed with the issuing company’s digital certificate. It is uploaded once in the settings and the system signs every receipt with it.

What happens if the DGII rejects a receipt?

You see it on the invoice and in the electronic documents panel, with the reason the DGII returned. It is corrected and transmitted again; the log keeps both attempts.

What if the internet goes down?

The invoice is saved anyway and the e-CF stays pending. When the connection is back, the pending ones are retransmitted in a batch from the panel.

Can I keep using traditional NCFs?

The platform handles both while they coexist: NCF sequences and e-CF sequences, each with its authorization and usage in view.

Does it work if I have several companies?

Yes. Each company in the group has its RNC, certificate and sequences, and its receipts are issued and reported separately.

How do I sign up?

By volume: a plan based on how many electronic receipts you issue per month, on top of the Invoicing module. Write to us and we will tell you which one fits you.

Is Zyntello an authorized DGII provider?

Zyntello is in the process of being authorized by the DGII as an electronic invoicing service provider. Meanwhile, your company certifies as an issuer with its own digital certificate, in the DGII environments and on the same system you will invoice with.
