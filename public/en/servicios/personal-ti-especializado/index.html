In detail

What you notice using it every day

Technical fit is not the only fit

Someone excellent at a hundred-person company can sink at a ten-person one, where you have to do everything. We look at the context.

The stack matters as much as the level

Someone very good in another technology takes months to perform in yours. If the deadline is tight, that settles it.

A single specialist is a risk

If one person is the only one who understands a system, their departure is a crisis. Plan the handover from day one.

The good ones are not looking

Posting a job advert filters for who is available, not for who is best. Strong profiles have to be sought out.

The market salary is a fact

An offer below market does not attract anyone; well above it unsettles the team already there. We say so before the search starts.

The first few weeks decide

Most hires that fail do so because onboarding was handled badly, not because of the profile. That is why we stay alongside.
