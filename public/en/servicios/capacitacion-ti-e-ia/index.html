In detail

What you notice using it every day

Training before you use it does not stick

Whatever gets taught without a real problem in front of you is forgotten within days. It is better to train when there is already something to solve.

Explaining the limits of AI is part of the course

A team that over-trusts a tool which is sometimes wrong produces errors that look like certainties.

The one who already knows it all teaches the most

Training whoever already masters the system so they can train the rest is what keeps the knowledge inside the company.

The system's built-in help gets taught too

Knowing that every screen explains what it does saves half the queries: plenty of people have no idea that panel is there.

Short, repeated sessions

A whole day saturates. Several short sessions with real work in between stick far better.

We also train the ones who do not want it

Resistance is nearly always fear of looking bad in front of the team. A small group with no audience sorts it out.
