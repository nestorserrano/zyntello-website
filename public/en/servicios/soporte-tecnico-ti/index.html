What it solves

IT only gets noticed when it fails, and by then it is too late

While everything works, nobody thinks about the server or the backup. The problem is that most outages could have been seen coming: a disk that had been warning for months, a backup that was never tested, a network built for ten people that forty now use.

  • When something goes down, there is nobody to call
  • The backup exists but nobody has tried restoring it
  • The network was built for half the people there are today
  • Nobody knows what equipment there is or what state it is in
  • The cost is in halting the operation, not in fixing the machine
  • Each incident gets fixed and nothing is recorded

What is included

What you actually get

Agreed service levels

Response time is agreed around what each system costs your operation while it is down. A production server is not a printer.

Backups that get tested

A backup that was never restored is not a backup: it is an assumption. We verify that it can be recovered, how old the data will be and how long it takes.

Networks and servers sized properly

LAN, WiFi, file and application servers, built for the people you have today and the ones you will have.

Maintenance before the failure

Regular checks on what warns before it breaks: disks, temperature, free space, updates. It is the cheapest thing you can do.

An inventory of what you have

What equipment there is, where, under which licence and in what state. Without that, every purchase decision is made blind.

Every incident gets recorded

What failed, what was done and how long it took. That is what makes the pattern visible: anything that happens three times has an underlying cause.

In detail

What you notice using it every day

A backup is measured in recovery time

Not in whether it exists. How long it takes to be operating again and how much work is lost are the two questions, and almost nobody has them answered.

Anything that happens three times has a cause

That is why incidents get logged: the same failure fixed ten times costs more than fixing its origin once.

Office WiFi is not home WiFi

Forty devices on a home router work until they do not, and the symptom looks like «the internet is slow».

An unlicensed machine is a legal risk

That is why the inventory includes licences: a software audit does not warn you before it arrives.

The user with administrator rights

It is the most common way in for ransomware. Removing it costs one awkward conversation and saves a very bad month.

Remote and on-site support

Most of it is solved remotely, and faster. What needs hands goes on site, within the time that was agreed.

Both. Most of it is solved remotely, and faster; what needs hands goes on site.

Yes, workstations as well as servers and the network, with their inventory and their licences.

Yes: a direct presence in the Dominican Republic, Venezuela, Colombia, Guatemala and Costa Rica, and remote support for the rest of the region.

What happens with repeated incidents?

They all get logged precisely so it shows: anything that happens three times has an underlying cause that is cheaper to fix.
