In detail

What you notice using it every day

A late close is not an accounting problem

It is a decision problem: the numbers arrive once the decision has already been made without them. That is why we attack the re-keying, not typing speed.

A cost centre only measures what gets charged to it

If half the costs go unassigned, the result by area is a fiction. The first thing to do is measure how much is left out.

The 607 has to reconcile with the income statement

If the sales declared and the sales recorded do not match, one of the two is wrong — and both have been filed.

An accrual that is never reversed counts twice

The estimated cost is recognised and then the invoice arrives. If the accrual is not reversed, the month's result lies.

Tax accounting and management accounting differ

And that is normal. The problem is when nobody knows by how much or why: that gap has to be explainable.

Decide what to outsource, and decide it early

Having the whole accounting function outside is convenient until you need a number quickly. The boundary gets set at the start.
