What it solves

Production is reported on paper and analyzed when it's too late to fix

In many plants the shift ends, someone types up the production sheet and the next day you find out how much downtime there was and how much was lost. By then the cause can no longer be investigated. Industry 4.0 isn't about robots: it's about plant information arriving in time to act on it.

  • The shift's output is known the next day
  • Downtime is logged from memory, if at all
  • Nobody knows the real efficiency of each line
  • Scrap is discovered in the month-end inventory count
  • Tracing a defective lot takes days
  • The plant and the ERP live in separate worlds

What you actually get

Efficiency per line, in real time

Availability, performance and quality for each machine — the OEE — calculated from the machine's own data, not from the shift sheet.

Downtime with its cause

Every stop logged with its duration and reason, so you tackle the ones that cost the most time, not the ones people remember most.

Screens on the line showing the shift target and what's been produced so far. The operator sees the same thing as the manager, while there's still time to do something about it.

Lot traceability

From raw material to shipped product: which lot, which machine, which shift. A return gets traced in minutes.

Plant and ERP connected

People at the center (5.0)

Technology that takes the repetitive and dangerous work off the operator and gives them information to decide. The goal is a more resilient and sustainable plant, not a plant without people.

Common questions

Questions

Do I have to replace my machines?

No. Machines with no factory connectivity can be measured with external cycle, count or power sensors. Only what has no other option gets replaced.

What is OEE?

Overall equipment effectiveness: how much time the machine was available, how fast it produced and how much came out good. It's the metric that shows where the plant's capacity is being lost.

What's the difference between 4.0 and 5.0?

4.0 connects machines and data. 5.0 puts people at the center: technology that supports the operator, a more resilient plant and more sustainable production.

Does it connect to my ERP?

Yes. Production orders, material consumption and finished goods move between the plant and the system, whether it's Softland, Profit, ODOO or the Zyntello platform.

How long does a pilot take?

It depends on the line and how its machines connect. The assessment gives you the specific timeline before we start.

Does it help with certifications?

Lot traceability and production records are evidence required by standards like ISO 9001 or programs like AEO. The system generates it with no extra work.
