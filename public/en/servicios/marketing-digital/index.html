In detail

What you notice using it every day

Reach goes up with spending, always

That is why it is the favourite metric of reports that would rather you did not look at the result. It is useful for comparing creatives, not for justifying spend.

With no CRM you cannot attribute the sale

You have to be able to follow the enquiry until it buys. If the follow-up lives in the salesperson's head, the measurement stops at the click.

Search ranking is slow and lasting

Paid campaigns bring traffic tomorrow and switch off when you stop; search ranking takes months and stays. It is worth doing both, knowing that.

Response speed decides the sale

An enquiry answered within minutes converts far better than one answered the next day. It is usually the biggest room for improvement, and it is free.

A bad lead costs the same as a good one

That is why we measure quality and not just quantity: a campaign that brings a hundred leads who do not buy is worse than one that brings ten.

Not every channel works for everyone

In B2B, a social network may not bring a single customer. You test with a small budget before believing any general rule.

Common questions

Questions

Which metrics do you report?

The ones that reach the sale: enquiries by source and which ones bought. Reach goes up whenever you spend more, so it justifies nothing.

Do I need to have a CRM?

To attribute the sale, yes: you have to be able to follow the enquiry until it buys. If you do not have one, we set it up — the module is in the platform.

How long does search ranking take?

Months, and that is why it is combined with paid campaigns, which bring traffic tomorrow but switch off when you stop.

What budget do you start with?

A small one, and measured. You scale what returns, not the other way round.

Do you work on the website too?

Yes: a site that gets visits and generates no enquiries is an expensive brochure.

Does it work for B2B?

It depends on the channel, and you test before assuming: in B2B a social network may not bring a single customer while search ranking does.
